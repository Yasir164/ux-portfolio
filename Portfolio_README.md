# Yasir Afaque — UX Design Portfolio

> I came to UX design through engineering research — completing my MTech thesis on optimizing complex operational systems using genetic algorithms at National Kaohsiung Normal University, Taiwan, supported by a Ministry of Education scholarship. That background in systems thinking, data analysis, and problem-solving under constraints now directly informs how I approach UX — I design with both analytical rigour and genuine empathy for the people using the product. I bring that combination of analytical depth and design craft to every project — from user research and competitive analysis through to hi-fi prototypes in Figma.

**Mental health peer support app & responsive website**
Safe, anonymous, free peer support through moderated community circles, flexible mood tracking, and always-visible crisis resources. Designed as a social good project.

- Platform: iOS Mobile + Responsive Website
- Screens: 18 mobile + 12 web
- Focus: Social good · Mental health · Inclusive design · Anonymous UX

**Personal finance & budgeting app for everyone**
A budgeting app that adapts to how users actually earn money — with income modes for salary, allowance, freelance, and pension users. Fills a gap left by every existing competitor.

- Platform: iOS Mobile + Responsive Website
- Focus: Fintech · Data visualisation · Variable income · Shared budgeting
